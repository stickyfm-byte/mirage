What is
Mirage?

A dark fantasy narrative experience built inside Minecraft, where cinematic productions, immersive roleplay and worldbuilding come together in one evolving fictional universe.

Join the story Join the Discord ↗

A world with a history of its own

Mirage is set in an original medieval world shaped by kingdoms, political conflict, ancient civilisations and supernatural forces. Its story follows the lives, ambitions and rivalries of the people who live there.

It isn't a traditional survival server. Characters and events add to a fictional history that keeps growing, and what happens in one chapter carries into the next.

Kingdoms & factions

Rival powers, shifting alliances and old grudges.

Ancient civilisations

A past that still shapes the present.

Supernatural forces

Powers beyond the mortal world.

Living characters

Played by the community, remembered by history.

Two sides, one universe

Mirage works as a story and as a production. Each feeds the other.

The narrative

This is where the world develops. Characters meet, form alliances, fall into conflict and build relationships, adding to the history of the universe.

  • Some events are scripted and carefully organised
  • Others leave room for character interaction and roleplay
  • Every choice can leave a mark on the world

The production

This is where the stories are brought to life: cinematic recordings, scripted episodes, films and other audiovisual productions made from what happens in the world.

  • Cinematic recordings and scripted episodes
  • Films and other audiovisual productions
  • Actors, builders and crew working as one team

Beyond the mortal world

The universe reaches past the kingdoms of the living and into the afterlife.

The Mortal World

Kingdoms, factions and the everyday lives of the people who live in them. This is where history is made, one choice at a time.

The Afterlife

When a character dies, their experience is shaped by their history, actions and influence, using custom effects and animations. A shaper of history won't meet the same end as an ordinary inhabitant.

Part of the story

Mirage is built together. There is a place for different kinds of people.

Actors

Portray characters, take part in roleplay and star in productions.

Builders

Shape the kingdoms, realms and sets the story takes place in.

Production staff

Record, script and edit the cinematic side of Mirage.

Event participants

Join organised events and help write the next chapter.
