OFFICIAL SUPPORT PORTAL

Contact the Support Team

Use the portal to submit a support ticket, report an issue, or request assistance. Our team will reply through the support system.

What to include

  • Your Minecraft username and Discord name
  • What happened, and when it happened
  • Screenshots or a short clip, if you have them

Stay safe

Never share your password, verification codes, API keys, or account recovery details. Mirage staff will never ask for your password.
