ABOUT MIRAGE

More than a survival world.

Mirage Productions brings creators together to make Minecraft content, events and community experiences.

01 • THE WORLD

Build your place.

Start with a block, a base or an idea. Mirage is a shared Minecraft world where players can create their own corner of the story.

02 • THE STORY

Every choice leaves a mark.

Characters, rivalries, friendships and events give the SMP its personality. The world grows as the people inside it do.

03 • THE COMMUNITY

Watch it unfold.

Follow Mirage creators, catch new videos and join the Discord community to keep up with what is happening across the server.

04 • THE PRODUCTION

Made for creators.

Mirage Productions supports Minecraft productions with applications, updates, creator resources and a central community hub.
